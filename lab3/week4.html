Session 2 · Playtest + cheat round

The playtest rules 📋

🧪 Tester: play honestly, then cheat

1. How long before it got boring?
2. Where did you get stuck or confused?
3. Would you play it again tomorrow? Why?

🤐 Builder: only say "thank you"

No explaining. No defending. Just listen.

Swap and play · 10 min

Play at least 3 runs. Be honest. 🎮

💡 "I stopped after two runs" is useful feedback. Boring counts!
