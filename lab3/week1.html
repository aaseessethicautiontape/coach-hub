      🕶️ Mission 3

      What's on the bouncer's list 📋

      allow read: if true👀 Anyone can look at the city
      request.auth.uid == uid🪪 You can only save YOUR building
      score is int🔢 Whole numbers only
      score <= 3000🚫 No crazy scores
      allow delete: if false🧹 Nobody deletes anything
      ❓ Quiz 2 of 3

      Would the bouncer let it in? 🕶️

      1. You're signed in, saving YOUR score of 1,200

      2. Changing SOMEONE ELSE's score

      3. Saving a score of 12.5

      🕶️ Mission 3

      Heads up! Your game saves 2 things 📂

      📁 scores
      📄 your building your best score✅ on the list
      📁 runs
      📄 every game you play run 1, run 2, run 3…❌ forgot = kicked out
      ⚠️ The bouncer needs a rule for BOTH. Don't forget the runs!
